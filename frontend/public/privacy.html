Privacy Policy & Biometric Data Governance Statement

Comprehensive enterprise governance framework detailing personal data protection, biometric template safeguards, body composition telemetry, and privacy compliance for LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI Health Platform.

01. Introduction & Legal Scope

Welcome to LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI (hereinafter referred to as "the Platform", "LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI", "We", "Us", or "Our"). LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI delivers an integrated enterprise software-as-a-service (SaaS) ecosystem, IoT access controller middleware, biometric authentication infrastructure, body composition scanner integration modules, Point of Sale (POS) terminals, and mobile applications designed for health clubs, fitness centers, gym operators, personal trainers, and gym members worldwide.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs the collection, processing, storage, disclosure, transmission, and protection of Personal Data and Sensitive Biometric Data gathered through your interaction with our cloud software, physical hardware terminals (including eSSL eBioserver middleware and connected access control turnstiles), body composition scanners (such as InBody BIA devices), web services, and native mobile applications.

By registering for an account, accessing any gym facility utilizing LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI infrastructure, consenting to biometric enrollment (facial recognition or fingerprint verification), undergoing body composition scans, or downloading our mobile application, you explicitly acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the data processing practices detailed within this Policy.

Strict Enterprise Safeguards Commitment
LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI strictly adheres to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) alignment standards, and global data protection statutes. We NEVER sell biometric templates or health metrics.

02. Key Definitions & Legal Terms

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the following capitalized terms shall have the exact meanings ascribed below:

  • "Account": A unique authorized credential created for a Facility Owner, Staff Member, Personal Trainer, or Gym Customer to access the Platform.
  • "Biometric Data": Any personal data resulting from specific technical processing relating to the physical, physiological, or behavioral characteristics of a natural person, including facial mathematical feature vector hashes, facial geometry representations, and fingerprint ridge minutiae templates.
  • "Body Composition Telemetry": Quantitative physiological measurements gathered via Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA) or optical body scanners, including total weight, body fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass, total body water, visceral fat level, extracellular water ratio, basal metabolic rate (BMR), and segmental muscle distribution.
  • "Facility Operator / Owner": The commercial enterprise or gym business entity operating a physical fitness location that utilizes LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI software and hardware middleware to manage operations.
  • "Member / Customer": An individual registered with a Facility Operator who utilizes physical gym services, access control doors, body scanners, or mobile member portals.
  • "eBioserver Middleware": The physical or cloud SOAP/HTTP server layer communicating directly with eSSL biometric hardware terminals, turnstile relays, and facial recognition devices.
  • "Personal Data": Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("Data Subject"), as defined under Article 4(1) of the EU GDPR.

04. Biometric & Body Composition Data Policy

This Section constitutes our formal Biometric Information Privacy Policy under Illinois BIPA (740 ILCS 14/), Texas Business & Commerce Code (§ 503.001), Washington Rev. Code (§ 19.375.020), and EU GDPR Article 9 (Special Category Data).

4.1 Nature of Biometric Processing

When you enroll in biometric access control at a facility powered by LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI, your facial image or fingerprint is captured by an integrated terminal (such as eSSL AiFace, ERIS, ARION, or F-series devices). The raw image is immediately processed locally by the hardware or eBioserver middleware to extract key spatial nodal points, creating an encrypted mathematical vector (hash). We do not store unencrypted raw facial photo files or raw fingerprint images in our biometric database.

4.2 Written Consent Requirement

Prior to initial biometric enrollment, every member or staff member must provide explicit written or digital consent via our enrollment portal. Consent is voluntary. If a member declines biometric enrollment, Facility Operators are required to provide alternative access mechanisms, such as RFID keyfobs, mobile QR codes, or staff-assisted entry.

Biometric Retention & Automated Retention Limits
LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI automatically purges biometric vector templates from server memory and physical eBioserver caches within thirty (30) days of a member's membership termination, or when the member revokes consent, or within three (3) years of the individual's last interaction with the facility—whichever occurs first.

4.3 Body Composition Telemetry Handling

Body composition readings captured through integrated BIA scanners are processed solely to provide personal wellness insights and graphical progress tracking to the member and their assigned certified personal trainer. BIA telemetry is classified as sensitive personal health data and receives AES-256 encryption at rest.

05. Legal Bases for Processing (EU GDPR)

In accordance with GDPR Article 6 and Article 9, LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI processes Personal Data under the following lawful legal grounds:

  1. Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Necessary to fulfill our contractual obligation to provide gym management software, process billing transactions, and allow facility entry.
  2. Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 9(2)(a)): Required for processing special categories of data, specifically biometric vector templates and detailed physiological body composition scans.
  3. Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Necessary for facility physical security, preventing unauthorized multi-person entry (tailgating), fraud prevention, and system performance monitoring.
  4. Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Processing required to comply with statutory tax, financial accounting, labor law, and health authority mandates.

06. Methods of Data Collection

Information is collected through three primary channels:

  • Direct Input: Form submissions during membership registration, POS checkout transactions, trainer consultation notes, or mobile app profile configuration.
  • Hardware & IoT Integration: Real-time HTTP/SOAP webhook pushes triggered when a member scans their face/fingerprint at eSSL hardware terminals or steps onto an integrated InBody scale.
  • Automated Telemetry: Automatic logging of HTTP requests, IP addresses, JWT token exchanges, error stack traces, and session cookies during software interaction.

07. Purposes of Data Processing

LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI utilizes collected data exclusively for the following operational and legal purposes:

  • Executing real-time physical access control door unlocks and turnstile relay triggers.
  • Recording accurate member check-in logs and calculating facility peak-hour analytics.
  • Generating body composition trend reports, macro recommendations, and workout schedules.
  • Processing recurring membership dues, Point of Sale transactions, and trainer commission payouts.
  • Enforcing facility security, investigating unauthorized access, and auditing staff operations.
  • Sending transactional SMS notifications, billing reminders, and push notifications.

09. Data Security & Encryption Standards

LazyMonkey FIT CLUB AI implements defense-in-depth security architecture to safeguard sensitive health and biometric assets:

  • Encryption at Rest: All database instances, biometric vector stores, and backup volumes are encrypted using FIPS 140-2 validated AES-256 algorithm.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data moving between physical eSSL terminals, eBioserver middleware, FastAPI backend services, and web frontend clients is transmitted via TLS 1.3 (HTTPS/WSS) with forced Perfect Forward Secrecy.
  • Network Isolation: Biometric middleware servers operate inside isolated Virtual Private Clouds (VPC) protected by strict Web Application Firewalls (WAF) and zero-trust security groups.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict principle of least privilege ensures staff members only access member data relevant to their role.

10. Data Retention & Automated Purging Schedules

We store data only for as long as necessary to fulfill active service contracts or comply with legal requirements:

  • Active Account Data: Retained for the duration of the active membership agreement.
  • Biometric Hashes: Automatically deleted within 30 days of membership cancellation or consent revocation.
  • Body Composition Logs: Retained for historical progress tracking until member requests account deletion.
  • Financial Transaction Logs: Retained for seven (7) years to comply with statutory tax audit regulations.
  • Server Access Logs: Automatically rotated and purged after ninety (90) days.
