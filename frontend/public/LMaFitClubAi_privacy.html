01. Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to the LMaFitClubAi mobile application (package com.lazymonkeyai.fitclubai), the LazyMonkey AI gym cloud infrastructure, connected smart workout stations, turnstiles, and InBody body composition scanners operated by LazyMonkey AI ("we", "us", "our") and authorized facility operators.

This statement governs the collection, processing, encryption, retention schedules, and deletion rights of all personal, physiological telemetry, workout, location, and biometric verification data.

03. Biometric & Camera Data Safeguards

🔒 Hardware-Layer Vector Protection Standard
We enforce strict biometric governance under Illinois BIPA (740 ILCS 14/) and GDPR Article 9. Raw facial photographs are never saved in biometric matching engines. Instead, optical landmarks are translated into irreversible encrypted mathematical vectors.
  • No Sale or Sharing: Biometric templates are never sold, rented, or leased to any third party or advertising broker.
  • Encrypted in Transit: All facial recognition telemetry transmitted between devices and cloud servers is encrypted via TLS 1.3 tunnels.
  • Auto-Purge Schedule: Biometric vector hashes are purged within 30 days of membership termination or upon account deletion request.

04. Data Security & Encryption Standard

All personal data, health telemetry, and biometric logs collected by the LMaFitClubAi application are:

  • Encrypted in Transit: Enforced HTTPS/TLS 1.3 with SHA-256 certificate pinning for all API interactions.
  • Encrypted at Rest: Database volumes and biometric vector tables are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Only authorized trainers and branch administrators have scoped access to assigned customer fitness records.
